Patients who can't get through

Because of the high volume of calls, it is increasingly hard to respond to every request. If you haven't heard back after 24 to 48 hours, ask your primary care physician's office to contact us by the fax number or email above.

We can't respond to patient requests by email because of high volumes and privacy concerns. Thank you for understanding.

Urgent or emergency

Go to your local emergency department or a walk-in clinic. Dr. Power can always be paged through the hospital switchboard, and is happy to discuss your care with the physician seeing you.

Recently had surgery?

If you have questions about your recovery, Dr. Power designed the Western Urology Care Assistant to help. It uses only reliable sources, and can ease worry or make sure you get help when you need it.
