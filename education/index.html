Urology education

Clear, organized overviews of common urology conditions, how they are diagnosed, and how they are managed.

For education only. These guides do not replace advice from your own care team.

  • Releasing pelvic pain: a guide for patients

    Long-form guide, with a printable PDF

    An in-depth guide for men and women living with chronic pelvic pain, pelvic floor tension, chronic prostatitis or bladder pain syndrome: why tests are often normal, the cycle of tension, worry and pain, patient stories, and the daily practices and treatments that help people recover.

  • Male pelvic pain

    Chronic prostatitis and chronic pelvic pain syndrome: what it is, why tests are often normal, how treatment is matched to your symptoms, a symptom score you can track, and pelvic floor physiotherapy clinics in London and southwestern Ontario.

  • Urinary tract infections

    UTIs happen when bacteria enter and multiply in the urinary system. They often cause burning with urination, frequency or urgency, and lower abdominal discomfort. Diagnosis is based on symptoms and urine testing, and treatment usually involves antibiotics and supportive care.

  • Kegel exercises

    Kegel exercise training for men and women, used to treat a range of conditions that affect urinary function.

  • Getting ready for bladder removal surgery

    A 4-week home program to help you get stronger before radical cystectomy: walking, strength and breathing exercises, eating well, quitting smoking, support, and what to expect in hospital.

  • Prostate cancer treatment decision aid

    Find your risk group, see the options usually offered for it, compare side effects with simple pictures, and work out which trade-offs matter most to you. Ends with a printable summary to bring to your appointment.

  • Raised PSA: what happens next

    For men with a raised PSA: whether to keep testing at your age and health, when to monitor, when an MRI or biopsy makes sense, what the results mean, and a printable summary for your appointment.

  • Bladder treatments: BCG and chemotherapy

    For bladder cancer that hasn't grown into the muscle: your risk group, BCG and chemotherapy treatments placed in the bladder, what each treatment day is like, side effects and warning signs, and a calendar of your treatments and check-ups.
